Somebody, Anybody, Nobody

Questions about people, things and places in your life.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks the question on the card.

2. Student B answers with a full sentence: “Yes, there’s something I can’t eat: shrimp!” / “No, nobody called me.”

3. A asks one more question. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about special people, things and places. “Is there somebody you really admire? Is there anywhere you want to go this year?” Write the answers.
