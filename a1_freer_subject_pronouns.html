Who or What Is It?

Describe it with he, she, it or they. Can your partner guess?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and opens 🤫. Only A looks! Say three sentences. Use he, she, it or they — don’t say the word! “It is big. It is grey. It lives in Africa.”

2. Student B guesses: “Is it an elephant?” A: “Yes, it is!” / “No, it isn’t.”

3. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls, A guesses.
