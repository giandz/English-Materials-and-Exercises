Pass the Message!

One-on-one: you are the messenger. Get a message, answer, then pass it on.

How to play (teacher + student):

1. Roll 🎲. The teacher is the first person on the card and reads the message to you.

2. You answer: “OK, I’ll tell her.” or “Sure, I’ll let them know.”

3. Now the teacher is the person who gets the message. Give it to them: “Lily, Mom says dinner is ready.”

4. Press 👀 to check your answers. Then roll again.

Your people: Tell the teacher about three people in your life (a friend, a family member, a coworker…).

The teacher gives you messages for them: “Tell your sister the class is on Monday.” You answer: “OK, I’ll tell her.”

Change roles: Now you give the teacher messages for people in their life: “Please tell your son I like his drawing.” “Can you let your friends know about the party?”
