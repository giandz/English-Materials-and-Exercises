Yes, I Am!

Make a question with am, is or are. Answer with the truth!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and makes a question: “Is your mom happy today?”

2. Student B answers. It’s true! “Yes, she is.” / “No, she isn’t. She’s tired.”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Meet your partner. “What’s your name? Where are you from? What’s your job? When is your birthday? How are you today?” Write the answers.

Then say: “This is Ana. She is from Lima. She is a nurse.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
