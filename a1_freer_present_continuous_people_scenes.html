A1–A2 · Elementary

People scenes

Look at the scene and talk about the people. What are they wearing? What are they carrying or holding? What are they doing?

Ask your partner

    Useful language

    • Who is wearing … ? — … is.
    • What is … wearing? — He's / She's wearing … .
    • What is … holding / carrying?
    • The man / woman / boy / girl wearing … is … .
    • Is … sitting or standing? Is … smiling?

    Guessing game

    1. Choose one person. Don't say the name.
    2. Describe the person: clothes, things, actions.
    3. Your partner says who it is.
    Teacher's key: who is wearing what
