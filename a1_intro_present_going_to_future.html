A1

Simple Present vs. Going To Future

Habits now, plans for later

🗓️ Mia and Leo are talking about their weekend
present (habit) going to (plan/prediction) time expression
🧑‍🦱

Leo

What do you usually do on Saturdays?

🧑

Mia

I usually clean my room and relax. What about you?

🧑‍🦱

Leo

I usually go to the gym in the morning. But tomorrow is different — I'm going to visit my grandparents.

🧑

Mia

That's nice! Do you visit them every week?

🧑‍🦱

Leo

No, not every week. Usually once a month. What are your plans for the weekend?

🧑

Mia

I'm going to study English. I have a test next week.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

Good luck! Do you always study on weekends?

🧑

Mia

Not always. But this weekend I'm going to study a lot.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

After my grandparents' house, I'm going to buy a present. My sister's birthday is next month.

🧑

Mia

That's thoughtful! I usually call my parents on Sundays. Tonight I'm going to call them early to ask for gift ideas.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

Good idea! Oh look, it's going to rain.

🧑

Mia

Let's go inside!
