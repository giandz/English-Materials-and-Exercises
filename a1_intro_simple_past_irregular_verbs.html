Irregular Past Tense
Use the past form for finished activities. Irregular verbs do not end in -ed.
  • Common irregular verbs
    I went to the mountains.
  • She saw a beautiful lake.
  • We ate lunch at a café.
  • They had a great time.
More Irregular Verbs
  • He came home early.
  • I drank some water.
  • We read a book.
  • She took many photos.
