Prepositions of Place — Grammar Review

Quick reference, word order, and gap fill practice for prepositions of place.

Quick reference
in / on / at

The books are in the cupboard. The cat is on the sofa. She is at school.

under / above / behind

The dog is under the tree. The picture is above the sofa. The stop is behind the school.

next to / between / in front of

The bakery is next to the bank. Tom sits between Ana and Mia. The car is in front of the house.
