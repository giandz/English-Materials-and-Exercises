Where Are You Today?

You are in a city somewhere in the world. Call your partner. Ask and answer: Where are you? What time is it? What’s the weather like?

How to play
  1. Write your name. You get a city. 🎲 gives you a new city.
  2. Everyone needs the same time. One student reads their world code. The other students write it and press 🔗 Sync.
  3. Press ➕ Add player for each partner and write their name.
  4. Don’t show your screen! Ask your partner. Choose their city. Move the clock hands. Choose the weather words and the degrees.
  5. Press ✓ Check. Are you right?
