After the vacation
☕ Sara and Leo meet after their vacations
👩
Sara
Hi Leo! How was your vacation?
👨‍🦱
Leo
It was great! I went to the beach with my family.
👩
Sara
Nice! Did you stay in a hotel?
👨‍🦱
Leo
Yes, we stayed in a small hotel. I swam in the sea every morning.
👩
Sara
Did you take photos?
👨‍🦱
Leo
Yes, a lot! And we watched the sunset every evening.
👩
Sara
Did you eat local food?
👨‍🦱
Leo
Of course! I ate local food every day. What about you? Where did you go?
👩
Sara
I didn't go to the beach. I went camping with my sister.
👨‍🦱
Leo
Cool! Did you go hiking?
👩
Sara
Yes, we went hiking every day. And on Saturday, we rented bikes.
👨‍🦱
Leo
Did you buy souvenirs?
👩
Sara
No, I didn't buy souvenirs. But I visited a museum.
👨‍🦱
Leo
And did you sleep late?
👩
Sara
No, I didn't! But it was a great vacation.
