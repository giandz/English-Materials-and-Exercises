What Am I Doing?

Act it out. Can your partner guess?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and opens 🤫. Only A looks! Act it out — no words.

2. Student B guesses: “Are you cooking?” A answers: “No, I’m not.” / “Yes, I am!”

3. Press 👀 for help with the questions. Then change: B rolls, A guesses.
