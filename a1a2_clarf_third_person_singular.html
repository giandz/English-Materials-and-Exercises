Note: we use these titles with a family name. We say Mr. Smith. We don't say Mr. John. And remember: Mrs. Lopez = she.
Grammar: he, she, it
● noun● pronoun● negative● question
he / she / it
✓ Positive
Mr. Smithhe works in the city. (work → works)
My bikeit needs new tires. (need → needs)
Your sistershe studies a lot. (study → studies)
✗ Negative
Mrs. Lopezshe doesn't drink coffee. (do → does)
The storeit doesn't open on Sundays. (do → does)
? Question
Does your brotherhe play soccer? (do → does)
Does the busit stop here? (do → does)
