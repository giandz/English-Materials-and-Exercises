What’s the Matter?

Play the doctor and the patient — and talk about staying healthy.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🩺 Role-play: open 🤫 — only A looks! A is the patient: say what’s wrong. B is the doctor: ask questions and give advice with should / shouldn’t / must / mustn’t.

💬 Talk: answer the question. Then ask your partner.

2. Press 👀 for useful phrases. Then change roles.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about staying healthy. “What do you do when you have a cold? How many hours do you sleep? What do you do to stay healthy?” Write the answers.

Then say: “When Ana has a cold, she drinks hot tea with honey.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
