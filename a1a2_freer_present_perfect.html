A Life in Pictures

Look at the pictures of a person’s life. Talk about it: what has this person done? What hasn’t this person done?

How to play: work with a partner. Look at each picture and say a sentence: She has lived in Brazil. / He hasn’t skied. Your partner asks: Has she ever…? Press 👁️ to check. Press 🎲 for a new person.
