Conversation
☕ two friends catching up after a weekend
👩
A
How was your weekend? Did you do anything exciting?
👨
B
Not really. I watched a movie on Saturday, but it was really boring. I was so bored I fell asleep!
👩
A
Ha! What movie was it?
👨
B
I can’t even remember the name. My sister recommended it — I was so disappointed. She said it was exciting, but I didn’t think so at all.
👩
A
That’s annoying! I hate it when someone recommends something and it’s not good. What did you do on Sunday?
👨
B
I went to a new museum. It was really interesting! I was fascinated by the history section.
👩
A
Oh, I love that place! Were you surprised by anything?
👨
B
Yes! There was an exhibition about ancient buildings. I had no idea — it was completely surprising. I was also a bit confused by some of the modern art, though.
👩
A
Modern art can be very confusing! Did you get tired? Those museums are huge.
👨
B
Very! I was absolutely exhausted by the end. But it was satisfying — I felt satisfied that I’d seen something really worthwhile.
👩
A
Much better than a boring movie! Maybe next time we can go together. I’m interested in the science section.
👨
B
Yes, let’s! Although I have to warn you — the gift shop is very interesting. I was so embarrassed — I spent twenty dollars on postcards!
