🌊 Water & ground

Water makes rivers. Grass, dirt, sand and snow only change the colour of the ground — roads, places and routes stay the same. Drag from a square that already has that colour to erase it.

🧭 Path

Click a place chip to start a route.

🏷️ Label

Type a name, then click a square to place it. Click a placed label to remove it, or drag to move it.

📍 Pin

Pick a color, then tap a place on the map. Tap the same pin again to remove it.

❓ How to play

  • Pick a tool in the toolbar at the bottom, or press 1–7.
  • 🛣️ Road and 🌊 Water: click or drag on the map to draw. Drag over it again to erase.
  • In the 🌊 Water drawer you can also paint the ground: 🟩 grass, 🟫 dirt, 🟨 sand or ⬜ snow. It only changes the colour.
  • Tap 🛣️ Road again to switch to 🛤️ Railroad. Trains only go from one 🚉 train station to another.
  • 🏗️ Build: drag a place onto the map. Tap it to remove it, or drag it to move it. The last place you picked shows on the Build button — drag from the button to add another one.
  • ⚓ Ferry: put it next to water. Routes can sail from one ferry to another.
  • 🚇 Subway: routes can go from one station to any other station.
  • 🧭 Path, 🏷️ Label and 📍 Pin open a drawer with options. Tap the tool again to hide it.
  • 🚗 Cars, 🚌 buses, vans, 🏍️ motorcycles, trucks, 🚆 trains and 🚢 ships move around by themselves. Traffic stops at red 🚦 lights and at railroad crossings when the red lights flash. Trains obey rail signals beside the track (green go, yellow a train is coming, red stop); the signals with a white frame are chain signals in front of junctions, and blue on them means only some ways out are free. You can turn it off in ⚙️ Options.
  • 🔍 Zoom: use + and − at the top left of the map, the mouse wheel, or pinch with two fingers. To move around, drag with two fingers, with the right mouse button, or hold Space and drag. The square button shows the whole map again, and the corner button makes the map full screen (F).
  • ⚙️ Options: change the grid size, save your city to a file or open a saved one, or clear the grid.

Traffic

Cars, vans, pickups and motorcycles drive on the right between places and roads at the edge of the map; buses run between the edges and big places like the 🏫 school or 🏥 hospital, and semis only cross from one edge to another. If you change the streets, they look for another way, or turn round and go back. They stop at red lights and at flashing railroad crossings. Trains run between stations and track at the edge of the map; the track is split into blocks with rail signals, and chain signals (white frame) guard the junctions, so trains never crash or block each other. Ships sail along the water, under bridges, and call at ⚓ ferry terminals; when two meet head-on they both steer to starboard, and they go round ships that are stopped or in the way. It's only decoration.

Clear

Removes everything on the map, including labels and routes. Save first if you want to keep it.
