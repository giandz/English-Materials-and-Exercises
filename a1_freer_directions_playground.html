A1–B1 · Giving Directions

City Playground — 8×8

A free-form map for practicing directions out loud. No scoring — build any city you like!

How to play: drag a place chip or a special road tile from the pools below onto the grid (reusable). To draw plain roads or water directly, click or drag on the grid — dragging from an empty square paints, dragging from an existing tile of the same type erases it. Roads and rivers connect automatically into straights, corners, T-junctions, and crossings. A ⚓ ferry terminal placed next to water grows a dock, and a route that reaches one will sail across connected water to another terminal to continue. A 🚇 subway station connects straight to any other station, no water needed. Tap a placed building to remove it, or drag it to move it.
