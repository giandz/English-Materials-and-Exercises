Free Practice — Giving Directions (A1)

🏗️ Build

Places
Special road tiles

Drag a chip onto the grid, or tap it to drop it in the first empty square.

🌊 Water & ground

Water makes rivers. Grass, dirt, sand and snow only change the colour of the ground — roads, places and routes stay the same. Drag from a square that already has that colour to erase it.

🧭 Path

Click a place chip to start a route.

🏷️ Label

Type a name, then click a square to place it. Click a placed label to remove it, or drag to move it.

📍 Pin

Pick a color, then tap a place on the map. Tap the same pin again to remove it.

❓ How to play

  • Pick a tool in the toolbar at the bottom, or press 1–7.
  • 🛣️ Road and 🌊 Water: click or drag on the map to draw. Drag over it again to erase.
  • In the 🌊 Water drawer you can also paint the ground: 🏕️grass, ⛰️ dirt, 🏜️ sand or ☃️ snow. It only changes the colour.
  • Tap 🛣️ Road again to switch to 🛤️ Railroad. Trains only go from one 🚉 train station to another.
  • 🏗️ Build: drag a place onto the map. Tap it to remove it, or drag it to move it.
  • ⚓ Ferry: put it next to water. Routes can sail from one ferry to another.
  • 🚇 Subway: routes can go from one station to any other station.
  • 🧭 Path, 🏷️ Label and 📍 Pin open a drawer with options. Tap the tool again to hide it.
  • ⚙️ Options: change the grid size, save your city to a file or open a saved one, or clear the grid.
