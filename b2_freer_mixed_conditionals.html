The Butterfly Effect

One small change — a different life. Imagine it!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🦋 Past → now: imagine a different past. How would your life be today? “If I had grown up in a big city, I’d probably be more confident now.”

🧠 Always → past: imagine you were different. What would have happened before? “If I weren’t so shy, I would have talked to her at the party.”

2. Student B asks two follow-up questions: “Would you be happier?” “What would you have said?”

3. Press 👀 to see the forms. Then change: B rolls, A asks.
