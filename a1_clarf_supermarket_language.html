At the Supermarket — Clarification

The phrases you need to shop, ask for help, and pay.

At the supermarket you mostly need three things: say what you need, ask where it is, and pay.
Saying what you need

We need milk, bread, and cheese.

I have a coupon for the cheese.

We don't need much today.

Asking where something is

Where is the milk? — It is in aisle 3, next to the yogurt.

Do you have any apples?

Can you grab a bag for the apples?

At the cashier

Are we ready to pay now?

Is that everything today? — Yes, that is all.

Here is your receipt.

a cart is the big one you push; a basket is the small one you carry. An aisle is the row between the shelves. A receipt is the paper that shows what you paid.
Common mistakes
✗Where are the milk?
✓Where is the milk?
✗I want a bag, give me.
✓Could I have a bag, please?
✗How much are this?
✓How much is this?
✗We need a bread.
✓We need some bread.
✗I go to pay at the cashier man.
✓I'm going to pay at the checkout.
