Plans, Promises and Predictions

Talk about your plans. Help your partner. Say what you think will happen — and how sure you are.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks the question (or says the words in the card).

2. Student B answers:
📅 a plan: “I’m going to visit my sister.”
💡 a decision now: “I’ll get you some water.”
🔮 a prediction: move the slider — how sure are you? Say the sentence and say why: “It probably won’t rain. It’s the dry season.”

3. A asks one more question. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
