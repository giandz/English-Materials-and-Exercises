Irregular Past Verbs — Clarification

Some verbs do not take -ed. You have to learn these ones.

Most verbs add -ed in the past: walk → walked. But the most common verbs are irregular — they change shape completely, and there is no rule. You learn them one by one.
The verbs in the conversation
go → wentI went to the mountains.
see → sawI saw a beautiful lake.
take → tookI took many photos.
eat → ateI ate lunch at a café.
come → cameI came back to the hotel.
sleep → sleptI slept for an hour.
buy → boughtI didn't buy anything.
drink → drankI drank hot chocolate.
read → readI read a book. Same spelling, different sound: /riːd/ → /red/.
have → hadI had a great time.
The trap: questions and negatives
This is the part learners get wrong. After did or didn't, the verb goes back to its normal form. The past is already shown by did.
affirmative

I went to the mountains.

question

Did you go anywhere? — go, not went

negative

I didn't buy anything. — buy, not bought

Short answers

Did you take any photos? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

You never repeat the main verb in a short answer.
