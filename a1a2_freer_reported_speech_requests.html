Who Asked You?

Tell your partner what people asked or told someone to do.

How to play (groups of 3):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the words to Student B, like the person on the card. Student C does not look!

2. Student B tells Student C: “Mom asked Ben to close the door.” or “Dad told Lily not to run.”

3. Student C does the action (or mimes it!). Then change roles.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Think about this week. What did your family, your teacher or your boss ask you to do? What did they tell you not to do?

Tell your partner three things: “My mom asked me to buy bread. My boss told me not to be late.”
