Ask Me!

Make the question. Then answer about you!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Make a question with the words: What … like? + your city → “What’s your city like?”

2. Student B answers. It’s true! “It’s big and noisy.”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
