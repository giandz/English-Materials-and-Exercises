Do It Like This!

Act it out — in a special way. Can your partner guess what and how?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and opens 🤫. Only A looks! Act out the action in that way — no words.

2. Student B guesses: “You’re eating… angrily!” A: “Yes!” / “No — slowly!”

3. Then change: B rolls, A guesses. Score a point for the action and a point for the adverb!
