Present Perfect: I have done it

have / has + past participle — affirmative, negative and yes / no questions

Look: Sam’s life up to now

Brown pictures are in the past. The blue picture is Sam now.

Sam has lived in Mexico. He has lived in Spain. He has played the drums. When? We don’t say. It is his life, from the past up to now.

Three forms

✔️ = yes, it happened · ❌ = no, it didn’t happen (up to now) · ❓ = a question

Countries: been to · lived in · visited
Past participles

Regular verbs: + -ed. Irregular verbs: learn them!
