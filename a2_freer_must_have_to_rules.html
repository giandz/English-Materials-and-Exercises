Rules Everywhere

Signs, laws and life: what’s necessary, what’s not allowed, and what’s your choice?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🪧 Sign: say the rule: “You mustn’t take photos here.” B says where you can see the sign.

🌍 Your life: answer the question and compare: “In Venezuela, you don’t have to vote… In my job, I have to wear a uniform.” B asks one more question.

2. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about the rules in their life. “What do you have to do at work? What mustn’t you do? What don’t you have to do on weekends?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana has to start work at seven. She mustn’t wear jeans. She doesn’t have to work on Sundays.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
