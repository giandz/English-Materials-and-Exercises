A1

Simple Past vs. Simple Present

Habits now, events in the past

☕ Sam and Priya are catching up
present (habit) past (finished event) time expression
🧑

Priya

Hi Sam! What did you do last weekend?

🧑‍🦱

Sam

I visited my grandparents. We ate a big dinner together.

🧑

Priya

Nice! I usually visit my parents every Sunday. Last week I was busy.

🧑‍🦱

Sam

What do you usually do on Sundays?

🧑

Priya

I study English in the morning. Then I watch a movie in the evening.

🧑‍🦱

Sam

I watched a good movie two days ago.

🧑

Priya

Really? What did you watch?

🧑‍🦱

Sam

I don't remember the name. I lost my phone that night. It was strange!

🧑

Priya

Oh no! Did you find it?

🧑‍🦱

Sam

Yes, the next day. I usually drink coffee every morning. Yesterday I drank tea.

🧑

Priya

I bought a new coffee maker last month. Now I drink coffee every day too!

🧑‍🦱

Sam

Nice! I go to the gym every day. I didn't go yesterday.

🧑

Priya

Why not?

🧑‍🦱

Sam

I played soccer with my friends. It was fun!
