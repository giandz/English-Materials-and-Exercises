Chain of Plans

Finish the sentence. How sure are you? Then your partner continues the chain!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and finishes the sentence. Use the word on the card — it shows how sure you are: “If I get a pay rise, I’ll probably buy a car.”

2. Student B continues the chain with A’s result: “If you buy a car, you’ll drive to the beach every weekend!” Keep going: how long can you make the chain?

3. Press 👀 to see an example. Then roll again.
