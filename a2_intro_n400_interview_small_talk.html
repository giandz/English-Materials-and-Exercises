Conversation
🏛️ the waiting room at the immigration office, 9:40 a.m.

Tap 🔊 to hear the officer.

👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Daniela Paredes?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Yes, that’s me. Good morning.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Good morning, Ms. Paredes. I’m Officer Harris. How are you today?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
I’m fine, thank you. A little nervous!
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
That’s normal. Please follow me. Did you have any trouble finding the office?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
No, it was easy. I came by bus.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Good. Is it still raining outside?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Yes, a little. But it isn’t cold.
🚪 in Officer Harris’s office
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Here we are. Please remain standing and raise your right hand. Do you swear to tell the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Yes, I do.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Thank you. Please have a seat. May I see your green card, your passport and your state ID?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Sure. Here you are.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Thank you. Do you know why you are here today?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Yes. I’m here for my citizenship interview. I want to become a U.S. citizen.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Great. First I’m going to ask you some questions about your application. Then we’ll do the English test and the civics test. If you don’t understand a question, just tell me. OK?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Sorry — could you repeat the last part, please?
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Of course. If you don’t understand a question, tell me.
👩‍🦱
Daniela
I understand. Thank you.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
Let’s start. What is your full name?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
Daniela Sofía Paredes.
👨‍💼
Officer Harris
And how long have you been a permanent resident?
👩‍🦱
Daniela
For six years. Since 2020.
