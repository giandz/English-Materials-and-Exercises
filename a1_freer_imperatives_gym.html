Do This, Don’t Do That!

Give instructions and rules. Your partner listens — and follows them!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

📋 How to…: give four steps: “First, put water in the pot. Then…” Student B mimes every step.

🪧 Rules: say three rules for the place: “Don’t talk. Turn off your phone…”

2. Press 👀 for ideas. Then change: B rolls, A listens.
