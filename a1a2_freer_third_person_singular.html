Meet Mr. Smith

Choose the title. Then talk about the person.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Choose the title — Mr., Ms., Mrs., Miss or Dr. — and say a sentence: “Dr. Chen gets up at five.”

2. Student B asks one more question: “Does she drink coffee?” A answers — any answer! “Yes, she does. She drinks three cups!”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A asks.
