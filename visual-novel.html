✍️ How a scene is written

The scene is the JSON in this page’s #vnScript: a backdrop, a cast of house characters (as saved by the poser) and a list of lines. Each line names who speaks and what they say, and can also set where they stand (left, center, right), the shot (full, medium, closeup), a pose (any pose preset, including the emotion ones such as em_joy), a face (emotion) or other details (look). stage brings other characters in, moves them, or takes them away with null. What a line sets stays until a later line changes it.

A character can change pose or face in the middle of a line: put a cue in the text where it should happen, such as "Music? {pose:em_surprise} Wow! {emotion:joy} I love music." A cue is {pose:…}, {emotion:…} or both, {pose:em_joy emotion:sadness}. It is not shown or said; it happens when the text reaches it, and what it sets stays.

Pose changes are eased with the keyframe editor’s easings. "easing" (an easing id such as quad, sine, back, elastic or bounce, or {"out":…, "in":…} for the two ends) and "moveTime" (seconds) set them for the scene, and any line can set its own for the moves it starts.

Keys: Space / Enter / → next (or finish the line), ← back, Home start again.
