Passive Voice — Procedural English — Quick Reference

Talking about rules, requirements, and official processes

The idea: in official and legal English, we often focus on the rule or process, not on who is enforcing it. be + past participle lets us do that: you are required to..., documents are needed to...
Common patterns
PatternExample
be required to + verbYou are required to bring your ID.
be expected to + verbYou are expected to answer honestly.
be asked to + verbYou will be asked to sign the form.
be allowed to + verbYou are not allowed to bring a phone inside.
be needed to + verbA valid ID is needed to confirm your identity.
Talking about a process over time

Past: Your fingerprints were taken last month.

Present: A photo ID is needed for the interview.

Future: You will be notified by mail.  ·  You will be asked some questions.

Modal: Six questions must be answered correctly.  ·  The form must be signed.

Active vs. passive

Active (who does it matters): The officer reviews your application.

Passive (the process matters, not who does it): Your application is reviewed.

Common mistakes
✗You require to bring your ID.
✓You are required to bring your ID.
✗The documents need to submitted.
✓The documents need to be submitted.
✗You was interviewed yesterday.
✓You were interviewed yesterday.
✗The application is requiring more documents.
✓More documents are required for the application.
