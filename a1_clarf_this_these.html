This, That, These, Those — Clarification

One thing or more than one? Near you or further away?

We use this and these for things near us, and that and those for things further away.
The four words
thisone thing, near — This is a sofa.
thesemany things, near — These are cushions.
thatone thing, far — That is an armchair.
thosemany things, far — Those are shelves.
Asking and answering

What is this? — It is a lamp.

What are these? — They are mugs.

What is that? — It is an oven.

What are those? — They are plates.

this / that go with is. these / those go with are, and the noun takes -s: a mug → two mugs.
a or an?
Use a before a consonant sound — a chair, a fridge, a towel. Use an before a vowel sound — an armchair, an oven, an alarm clock.
