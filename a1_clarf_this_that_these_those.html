This, That, These, Those — Quick Reference

Talking about things that are near and things that are far

The idea: we use a different word depending on whether something is near or far, and whether it is one thing or more than one.

In a sentence

This is my phone.  ·  That is your phone.

These are my apples.  ·  Those are your apples.

Use is with this/that. Use are with these/those.

Asking questions

What is this? — It's a phone.  ·  What is that? — It's a phone too.

What are these? — They're apples.  ·  What are those? — They're apples too.

Common mistakes
✗This are my shoes.
✓These are my shoes.
✗Those is my book.
✓That is my book.
✗I like that apples.
✓I like those apples.
✗This is my parents.
✓These are my parents.
