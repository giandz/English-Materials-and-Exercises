How Much? How Many?

A conversation about quantities, using "much", "many", "a few", "a little", and more.

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

🛒 two roommates, checking the fridge before shopping
Conversation

Quick reference
Use how much and much/a little with things you can't count (milk, sugar). Use how many and many/a few with things you can count (eggs, apples).
