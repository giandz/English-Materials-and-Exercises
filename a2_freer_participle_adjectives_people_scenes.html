Who's who?

Talk about the people in the scene. Say which person you mean with a verb-ing phrase: the woman wearing…, the man holding…, the boy sitting…

Roll the dice for something to say.
Rolls: 0

Useful language

  • Who's the man / woman / boy / girl …ing … ? — That's … .
  • Which one is … ? — He's / She's the one …ing … .
  • The woman wearing … is … .
  • Do you mean the man holding … or the man carrying … ?
  • No, that isn't … . … is the girl sitting … .

Three games

  1. Ask: read the question on the card. Your partner finds the person and says the name.
  2. Describe: the card gives you a name. Describe that person without saying the name. Your partner says who it is.
  3. True or false? read the sentence. Your partner checks the picture and corrects it if it is wrong.
Teacher's key: one sentence for each person
