True or False?

Talk about your past. Is your partner telling the truth?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and makes a sentence. It can be true or false! “Last weekend I played soccer.”

2. Student B asks one question: “Did you play with friends?” Then B says: “True!” or “False!”

3. A says the answer: “Yes, I did.” / “No, I didn’t. I stayed at home!” Press 👀 to check. Then change.
