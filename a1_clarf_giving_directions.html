Giving Directions — Visual Reference

Common expressions for giving directions.

Direction phrases
Turn left / Make a left
Make a left at the light.
Go straight
Go straight for two blocks.
Turn right / Make a right
Turn right on Main Street.
Along
Walk along the river.
Go across / Cross
Cross the street at the crosswalk.
Turn around
Turn around and go back.
Go past
Go past the bakery, then stop.
Go through
Go through the tunnel.

Directions are imperatives too — no subject. The preposition is what turns a movement into a place: turn left vs turn left at the park.
Location phrases
Opposite (🇬🇧)/ Across from (🇺🇸)
The bank is across from the park.
Next to
The cafe is next to the bakery.
Between
It's between the pharmacy and the shop.
On / At the corner
It's on the corner of 5th and Main.
It's on the left
The clinic is on the left.
It's on the right
The bakery is on the right.
Around the corner
It's just around the corner.
Common mistakes
✗You go straight two blocks.
✓Go straight for two blocks.
✗Turn at the left.
✓Turn left.
✗Cross of the bridge.
✓Cross the bridge.
✗It is in the corner.
✓It is on the corner.
✗Go to straight.
✓Go straight.
