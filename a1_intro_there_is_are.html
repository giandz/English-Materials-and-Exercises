There is / There are — Conversation

Count and noncount nouns in everyday talk

🛒 Ava and Leo are checking the kitchen before a picnic
there is / are noun negation
👱‍♀️

Ava

Let's make sandwiches for the picnic. What do we have?

👨‍🦰

Leo

There's some bread on the counter.

👱‍♀️

Ava

Good. Is there any cheese in the fridge?

👨‍🦰

Leo

Yes, there is. And there are three eggs too.

👱‍♀️

Ava

Perfect. What about vegetables? Are there any tomatoes?

👨‍🦰

Leo

Hmm... there aren't any tomatoes, but there's some lettuce.

👱‍♀️

Ava

That's fine. There's no mayonnaise, right? I checked yesterday.

👨‍🦰

Leo

Actually, there is some — I bought a new jar.

👱‍♀️

Ava

Great! And for dessert — there are some strawberries in the bowl, aren't there?

👨‍🦰

Leo

Yes, and there is some chocolate in the drawer too!

👱‍♀️

Ava

One more thing — there isn't any water in the bottles. Can you fill them?

👨‍🦰

Leo

Sure! There are two bottles here — I'll fill them now.

More Examples — Other Topics

The same grammar with furniture and places in a city

🛋️ Furniture

  • There's a sofa next to the window.
  • There are two chairs at the table.
  • There isn't a lamp in the bedroom.
  • There aren't any shelves in the hallway.
  • There's no furniture in the new office yet.

🏙️ Places in a City

  • There's a park near my house.
  • There are many restaurants downtown.
  • There isn't a train station in this town.
  • There aren't any tall buildings here.
  • There's no traffic on Sunday mornings.
