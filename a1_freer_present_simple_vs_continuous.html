Usually… But Today!

Every day is the same — but not today! Say what is different.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Say the sentence: “My mom usually walks to work, but today she’s driving.”

2. Student B asks: “Why?” A gives a reason — any reason! “Because it’s raining!”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A asks.
