The Wishing Well

Throw a coin 🪙 — and make a wish about your life.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the situation (📍 = what’s really true). There are three kinds of card:

🌙 Now: “I wish I had…” · ⏪ Regret: “I wish I hadn’t…” · 😤 Annoying habit: “I wish he would…”

2. Make the wish and say why. Student B asks one question: “What would you do if you had one?” / “Why did you do it?”

3. Press 👀 for an example. Then change: B rolls, A asks.
