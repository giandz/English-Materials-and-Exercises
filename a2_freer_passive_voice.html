Who, Where, When?

A quiz about the world. Ask and answer with the passive.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the question to B: “What language is spoken in Brazil?”

2. Student B answers with the passive: “Portuguese is spoken in Brazil.” Not sure? A reads the three options.

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls and asks. Who gets more right?
