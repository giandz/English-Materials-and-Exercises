At the Places

Nine short conversations using common phrases for different places in town.

Place vocabulary Useful phrase
At the airport ✈️
A passenger is going through airport security.
🧳
Passenger
Excuse me. Where is gate 12?
🛂
Airport worker
It is at the end of this hall.
🧳
Passenger
Thank you.
🛂
Security officer
Can I see your boarding pass?
🧳
Passenger
Yes, here it is.
🛂
Security officer
Thank you. Please put your liquids in this tray.
At the museum 🏛️
A visitor is talking to a museum guide.
👩
Visitor
This painting is beautiful.
🧑‍🏫
Museum guide
Thank you. This painting is from 1890.
👩
Visitor
Can I take a photo?
🧑‍🏫
Museum guide
Yes, but you can’t use flash photography here.
👩
Visitor
Okay. Where is the dinosaur exhibition?
🧑‍🏫
Museum guide
The dinosaur exhibition is upstairs.
At the library 📚
A student wants to borrow some books.
🧑
Student
Excuse me. Where is the children’s section?
👩‍💼
Librarian
It is on the first floor.
🧑
Student
Can I borrow these books?
👩‍💼
Librarian
Yes, but you need a library card to borrow books.
🧑
Student
I have my card.
👩‍💼
Librarian
Great. Please return this book by Friday.
At the restaurant 🍽️
A customer is having dinner.
🧑‍🍳
Waiter
Good evening. Would you like to see the menu?
👩
Customer
Yes, please.
🧑‍🍳
Waiter
Here you are. What would you like to order?
👩
Customer
I’d like the chicken and rice, please.
🧑‍🍳
Waiter
Of course.
👩
Customer
Excuse me. Can I pay, please?
🧑‍🍳
Waiter
Certainly. Here is your bill.
At the hospital 🏥
A patient has an appointment with a doctor.
👩‍💼
Receptionist
Hello. What is your date of birth?
🤒
Patient
It is 12 May 2001.
👩‍💼
Receptionist
Thank you. Please wait in the waiting room.
🤒
Patient
Okay.
👨‍⚕️
Doctor
Hello, Maria. The doctor will see you now.
🤒
Patient
Thank you.
At the supermarket 🛒
A customer is shopping for food.
👩
Customer
Excuse me. Where can I find bread?
🧑‍💼
Worker
The bread is in aisle five.
👩
Customer
Thank you. And where is the checkout?
🧑‍💼
Worker
It is near the entrance.
👩‍💼
Cashier
Hello. Do you need a shopping bag?
👩
Customer
Yes, please.
At the cinema 🎬
A customer is going to watch a film.
👨
Customer
Hello. What time does the film start?
🎟️
Cinema worker
The film starts at 7:30.
👨
Customer
Great. Where is Screen 4?
🎟️
Cinema worker
Screen 4 is on the left.
👨
Customer
Thank you. A large popcorn, please.
🎟️
Cinema worker
Sure. That is five dollars.
At the hotel 🏨
A guest is checking in at a hotel.
👩‍💼
Receptionist
Welcome to the hotel. Here is your room key.
🧳
Guest
Thank you. What time is breakfast?
👩‍💼
Receptionist
Breakfast is served from 7:00 to 10:00.
🧳
Guest
Great.
👩‍💼
Receptionist
What time would you like to check out?
🧳
Guest
At 10:00, please.
At the pharmacy 💊
A customer needs medicine for a headache.
👩
Customer
Hello. Do you have anything for a headache?
🧑‍⚕️
Pharmacist
Yes. These tablets can help.
👩
Customer
How do I take them?
🧑‍⚕️
Pharmacist
Take these tablets after food.
👩
Customer
Can I buy this stronger medicine too?
🧑‍⚕️
Pharmacist
You need a prescription for this medicine.
Quick reference
Study tip: Read each conversation aloud with a partner. Then change one detail: the gate number, the food order, the film time, the medicine, or the room number.
