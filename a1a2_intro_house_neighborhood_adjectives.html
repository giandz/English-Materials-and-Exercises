🏘️ What is your neighborhood like?

Talk with a partner. Use the words on this page.
  • Is your neighborhood quiet or noisy? Is it safe?
  • Is it near the city center, or far from it?
  • Are the houses new or old? Are they big or small?
  • Are the houses cheap or expensive?
  • Are your neighbors friendly?
  • What is your house or apartment like? Is it bright or dark?
You can say
A: What is your neighborhood like?
B: It’s quiet and safe, but it’s far from the city center.
A: Are the houses big?
B: No, they’re small, but they’re cheap. And the neighbors are very friendly!
