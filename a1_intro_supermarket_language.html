At the Supermarket

A conversation using supermarket language

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

🛒 Mia and Jake are grocery shopping
🧑‍🦱

Jake

Do you have the shopping list?

🧑

Mia

Yes, it's right here. We need milk, bread, and cheese.

🧑‍🦱

Jake

Should we get a cart or a basket?

🧑

Mia

Let's use a basket — we don't need much today.

🧑‍🦱

Jake

The milk is in aisle 3, next to the yogurt.

🧑

Mia

Great, let's go. Oh, I have a coupon for the cheese!

🧑‍🦱

Jake

Nice, that'll save us some money. Can you grab a bag for the apples?

🧑

Mia

Sure. Are we ready to pay now?

🧑‍🦱

Jake

Yes, let's go to the cashier.

🧑‍💼

Cashier

Hi! Is that everything today?

🧑‍🦱

Jake

Yes, that's all, thanks.

🧑‍💼

Cashier

Here's your receipt. Have a great day!

🧑‍🦱

Jake

Thanks! You too.
