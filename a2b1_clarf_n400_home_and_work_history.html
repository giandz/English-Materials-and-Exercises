Still true now → present perfect: have / has + past participle. I’ve lived here since 2023. I’ve worked there for two years.

Finished → past simple: I lived on Lake Street for three years. I worked at Casa Linda from 2022 to 2024.

The question: How long have you lived there? (now) · How long did you live there? (finished)

for + a length of time (for two years) · since + the start (since 2023, since June) · from … to … for finished times.
