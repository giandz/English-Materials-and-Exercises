wpm

Mouth shapes start at the playhead, on the Mouth timeline, and replace what was there for that stretch. About 120 wpm is slow and clear, 150 natural, 180 or more fast. Pauses rest on the mouth the character has at the playhead (a smile stays a smile).

Look changes go on

Clothes, colours, items and props are keyframed: a change holds until the next keyframe, with no in-between. Choose “Every keyframe” to change the whole animation at once.

🧍 Pose presets

Joint angles and turns move smoothly between keyframes. Everything else (face, clothes, items…) changes at the keyframe.
