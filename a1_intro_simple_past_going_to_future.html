Simple Past vs. Going To Future

Completed actions vs. Future plans

☕ Emma and Lucas are catching up at a cafe
simple past (completed) going to (plan/intention) time expression
👩

Emma

Hey Lucas! How was your vacation?

🧑‍🦱

Lucas

It was amazing. I went to the beach last week.

👩

Emma

Nice! I stayed in the city. But next month, I'm going to travel to Costa Rica!

🧑‍🦱

Lucas

That's exciting! I visited Costa Rica two years ago. You're going to love it.

👩

Emma

Thanks! I bought my plane tickets yesterday. Where are you going to go for your next trip?

🧑‍🦱

Lucas

I'm going to stay home. I spent too much money last week!

👩

Emma

Haha! I understand. Are you going to do anything fun this weekend?

🧑‍🦱

Lucas

Yes, I'm going to watch a soccer game with my brother. My favorite team won their match on Tuesday.

👩

Emma

Have fun! I'm going to start packing my bags tonight.
