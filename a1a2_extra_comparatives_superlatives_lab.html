⚖️ Leo is taller than Ana. · 🏆 Leo is the tallest of the three.

The pattern

⚖️ Comparative — two things:

A+ is+ adjective-er/ more adjective+ than+ B

🏆 Superlative — three or more things:

A+ is+ the+ adjective-est/ most adjective (+of / in …)
1 syllabletall → taller → the tallestbig → bigger · nice → nicer
2 syllables + yhappy → happier → the happiesty → i
Longexpensive → more expensive → the most expensive3+ syllables, or 2 not ending in y
Specialgood → better → the bestbad → worse → the worst · far → farther → the farthest

Never both: taller or more tall? → taller. Never “more taller” or “the most tallest”.

than, not “that”: Leo is taller than Ana.
