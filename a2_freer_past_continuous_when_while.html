And Then…!

Make crazy stories together. Then find out what your partner was doing yesterday.

⚡ past simple⏳ past continuouswhen · while
Story dice

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and makes one sentence with ⏳ and ⚡: “I was taking a shower when the lights went out.”

2. Student B asks: “Oh no! What did you do then?” A says one more sentence. B asks one more question.

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A asks.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you: what were you doing?

Ask your partner: “What were you doing yesterday at 8 a.m.? … at 1 p.m.? … at 7 p.m.?” Write the answers. Then ask: “Did anything happen? What happened while you were …?”

Then tell the class: “At 8 a.m., Ana was driving to work when her phone rang.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
