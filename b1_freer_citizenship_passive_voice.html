A speaking activity about official processes and requirements

🎭 Role play: mock interview
Work in pairs. One person is the officer, the other is the applicant. Act out a short interview. Then switch roles.
Officer
Explain the process to the applicant using passive forms.
  • You are required to…
  • You will be asked to…
  • A valid ID is needed to…
  • You will be notified by…
Applicant
Ask questions and respond to the officer.
  • What am I required to bring?
  • When will I be notified?
  • Is a translator allowed?
  • What happens if I'm not approved?
Useful phrases
to help you answer
You are required to… You will be asked to… … is needed to confirm… You are expected to… You will be notified by… The application was approved… You are not allowed to… The form must be signed…
