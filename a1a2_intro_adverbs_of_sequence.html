Grandma Lucia's recipe

Read the conversation between Mia and her grandmother.

Vegetable Soup

👧MIA

Grandma, can you show me how to make your vegetable soup?

👵GRANDMA

Of course, mia cara! It's easy. First, we peel the carrots and the potatoes.

👧MIA

Okay, I'm peeling now. What's next?

👵GRANDMA

Next, chop the onion, the carrots, and the potatoes into small pieces.

👧MIA

Like this?

👵GRANDMA

Perfect! Then, we fry the onion in a little oil for two minutes.

👧MIA

It smells wonderful already!

👵GRANDMA

After that, add the carrots, the potatoes, and the water. Boil everything for ten minutes.

👧MIA

And after it boils?

👵GRANDMA

After that, we simmer the soup for thirty minutes. Stir it two or three times.

👧MIA

What about the salt?

👵GRANDMA

Add a little salt and pepper before you simmer it.

👧MIA

And finally?

👵GRANDMA

Finally, pour the soup into bowls and grate a little cheese on top. Buon appetito!

👧MIA

Thank you, Grandma! Now I can make it for the whole family.
