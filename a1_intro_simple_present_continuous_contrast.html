A1

Simple Present vs. Present Continuous

Habits vs. actions happening right now

📱 Mia and Leo are on a phone call
present (habit) continuous (now) time expression
🧑‍🦱

Leo

Hi Mia! What are you doing right now?

🧑

Mia

I'm cooking dinner. What about you?

🧑‍🦱

Leo

I'm watching TV at the moment. I usually cook on Mondays, but I'm too tired today.

🧑

Mia

I understand! I usually eat with my family every evening.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

That's nice. Are you making something special today?

🧑

Mia

Yes! I'm making pasta right now. I usually make rice, but I want something different.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

Sounds good! I usually exercise in the morning, but I'm just relaxing right now.

🧑

Mia

Good idea! Enjoy your evening.

🧑‍🦱

Leo

You too! Talk soon.
