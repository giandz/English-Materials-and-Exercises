The Citizenship Interview — Role Play

Work with a partner. One person is the officer. One person is the applicant. Then change! At the real interview, always give your true answers.

You can always say
Could you repeat that, please? Could you speak more slowly, please? Sorry, what does “…” mean? Do you mean …? Let me think, please. Sorry, I’m a little nervous.
1 · Small talk: meet your partners

You are the officer. Say hello and ask the four questions. Write your partner’s answers. Then meet a new partner.

🚌 How did you get here today? 🌦️ How is the weather outside? 😊 How are you feeling today? 🏙️ How long have you lived in this city?
2 · Roll the dice: you are the officer

Roll the dice. Your partner hears the question and answers. Press 🙈 to hide the words: at the interview you only listen! Then pass the dice.
