N-400: Has Anything Changed? — Role Play

Work with a partner. One person is the officer. One person is the applicant. Then change!

You can say
I’d like to tell you about a change. Two things have changed. I’ve moved / changed jobs / … I moved in … / I left on … / I started in … Here is … No, nothing has changed. Nothing else has changed.
1 · Role cards

The applicant rolls the dice and gets a card. It shows what has changed since you filed. Don’t show it to the officer! The officer starts: “Has anything changed since you filed your application?” Then the officer asks for the details and the papers.

Press the dice to get your card.
Cards: 0
2 · The officer’s questions

Roll the dice. Your partner hears the question and answers about their card: first the news, then the details. Then pass the dice.

Press the dice to start.
Questions: 0
3 · What has changed this year?

Now talk about real life. Ask your partners: “What has changed in your life this year?” Write the news and one detail. Only share what you want to share!
