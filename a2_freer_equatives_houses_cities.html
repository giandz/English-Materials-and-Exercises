Which Is Better?

Compare homes and places. Do you and your partner agree?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Choose a symbol: < = or >. Say your sentence: “A house isn’t as cheap as an apartment.”

2. Student B agrees or doesn’t agree: “I agree.” / “I don’t think so. I think it’s just as cheap.” Say why!

3. Press 👀 to see three example sentences. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
