Phrases for Places

Airport, library, café, pharmacy … In every place you ask, you understand the answer, and you are polite.

Four ways to ask

Read the questions from the conversations.

Where is the checkout?📍 asking for a place
Can I borrow these books?🙋 asking for permission
Do you have anything for a headache?🛍️ asking if they have it
What time does the film start?🕖 asking about time
Answers you will hear
✈️ AirportIt is at the end of this hall. · Can I see your boarding pass?
🏛️ MuseumThe dinosaur exhibition is upstairs. · You can’t use flash photography here.
📚 LibraryYou need a library card to borrow books. · Please return this book by Friday.
🍽️ RestaurantWhat would you like to order? · Here is your bill.
🏥 HospitalWhat is your date of birth? · Please wait in the waiting room.
🛒 SupermarketThe bread is in aisle five. · Do you need a shopping bag?
🎬 CinemaThe film starts at 7:30. · Screen 4 is on the left.
🏨 HotelHere is your room key. · Breakfast is from 7:00 to 10:00.
💊 PharmacyTake these tablets after food. · You need a prescription for this medicine.

Where is + place? Where is the checkout? · Where is gate 12? (no the before a number: gate 12, Screen 4)

Can I + verb? Can I borrow these books? (no to)

Do you have + thing? Do you have anything for a headache?

What time does + thing + verb? What time does the film start? (start — not starts)

Be polite. Start with Excuse me. End with please and thank you. Say I’d like … and Could I have …, please? — not I want or Give me.
