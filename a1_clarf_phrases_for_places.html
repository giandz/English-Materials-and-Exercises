Phrases for Places — Clarification

The same three moves work in every place: ask, answer, thank.

Wherever you are — airport, library, café, pharmacy — you usually need to do the same three things: ask for what you want, understand the answer, and be polite.
Three ways to ask

Where is the checkout? — asking for a place

Can I borrow these books? — asking for permission

Do you have anything for a headache? — asking if they have it

What time does the film start? — asking about time

Answers you will hear
✈️ AirportCan I see your boarding pass? · Where is gate 12?
🏛️ MuseumThe dinosaur exhibition is upstairs. · You can't use flash photography here.
📚 LibraryYou need a library card to borrow books. · Please return this book by Friday.
🍽️ RestaurantWhat would you like to order? · Here is your bill.
🛒 SupermarketThe bread is in aisle five. · Do you need a shopping bag?
🎬 CinemaThe film starts at 7:30. · Screen 4 is on the left.
🏨 HotelHere is your room key. · Breakfast is served from 7:00 to 10:00.
💊 PharmacyTake these tablets after food. · You need a prescription for this medicine.
Two small words do a lot of work. Excuse me starts the conversation; please and thank you end it. Without them the same question can sound rude.
