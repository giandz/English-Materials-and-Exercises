Can and Can't — Quick Reference

Talking about what you can and can't do

The idea: we use can to say we are able to do something, and can't to say we are not able to do it. The form is the same for every person.
Statements

I can swim.  ·  She can cook.  ·  They can dance.

I can't swim.  ·  She can't cook.  ·  They can't dance.

Questions

Can you swim? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

Can he play the guitar? — Yes, he can. / No, he can't.
