House Detectives

Somebody is somewhere in the house. Read the clues. Where are they? What are they doing?

How to play (pairs):

1. Roll 🎲 and read the clues together.

2. Talk: where can’t they be? Where might they be? Where must they be? “She can’t be in the backyard — it’s raining. She might be in the kitchen …”

3. Agree on one answer and say how sure you are (for example 75%: “She’s probably cooking.”).

4. Press 👀 to see the answer. Then roll again.
