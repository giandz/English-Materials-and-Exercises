Have You Got…?

Find out about your partner’s life and things.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks the question on the card: “Do you have a pet?” or “Have you got a pet?”

2. Student B answers with a short answer and one more detail: “Yes, I do. I have a cat. Her name is Luna.”

3. A asks one more question: “What color is she?” Press 👀 to check. Then change.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about their family and things. “Have you got any brothers or sisters? Do you have a pet? What color eyes have you got?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana has got two sisters. She doesn’t have a pet.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
