Look: two kinds of message

Read the messages from the phone call. Look at the people.

1 · Direct: I talk to you

The two people are talking. The message is for the listener — or for the speaker. We use me, you, us.

2 · Relaying a message: A → B → C

The message is for a person who is not there. B is the messenger. We use him, her, them.
