The pattern
modal+ base verb — must, shall, would, had better, can’t, might
have to / need to / ought to / be able to+ base verb — these change: has to, had to, will be able to
🏋️ Obligationhave to · must · need topast: had to
🚫 / 🆓mustn’t = not alloweddon’t have to = not necessary
⚠️ Advicehad better · ought tohad better = or there’s a problem
💪 Abilitybe able towill be able to · was able to · haven’t been able to
🤝 Offers & ideasShall I…? · I’ll… · Let’s… · Why don’t we…?How about + -ing?
🔍 Deductionmust be ✔️ · might be 🤷 · can’t be ✗not “mustn’t be”

No “to” after a modal: You’d better hurry (not better to hurry) · Let’s go (not Let’s to go).

-ing after How about…? and Would you mind…? — How about ordering pizza? · Would you mind closing the door?
