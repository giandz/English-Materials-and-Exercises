What’s My Job?

Guess the job — and talk about the jobs in your life.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🤫 Guess: only A looks at the job. A says: “It’s a person who…” — but not the job! B guesses. B can ask: “Do you work outside? Do you wear a uniform?”

💬 Talk: answer the question. Then ask your partner.

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about work. “What do you do? What’s your dream job? What jobs do people in your family do?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana is an assistant. Her dream job is photographer. Her brother is a firefighter.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
