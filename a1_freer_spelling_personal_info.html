Let's Talk: Spell It Out

A short speaking activity about personal information

🗣️ Mingle — ask and spell
Talk to three classmates. Ask 2–3 questions from the cards, and ask them to spell their answer. Tap a card after you ask it.
1
What's your first name? How do you spell it?

🎭 Role play: at the front desk
Work in pairs. One person is filling in a form; the other is giving personal information. Practice asking for and correcting spelling.
Receptionist
Ask for your partner's information and check the spelling of anything tricky.
  • Can I have your name, please?
  • How do you spell that?
  • Is that spelled...?
  • Could you spell that again?
Visitor
Give your information. On purpose, let the receptionist guess your name wrong once, then correct them!
  • It's... (name)
  • It's spelled...
  • No, it's [word] with a [letter].
  • That's right!
