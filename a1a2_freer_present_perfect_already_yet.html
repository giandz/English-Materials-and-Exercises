My Week So Far

Ask and answer about jobs: done or not yet?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Roll 🎲. Student A looks at the card. It’s A’s list for today — and some numbers for this week.

2. Student B asks: “Have you fed the dog yet?” A answers: “Yes, I’ve already fed the dog.” or “No, not yet. I haven’t fed the dog yet.”

3. B asks about the numbers: “How many emails have you sent this week?” A: “I’ve sent seven emails.”

4. Press 👀 to check. Then change roles and roll again.
