What Did They Ask?

Tell your partner what people asked.

How to play (groups of 3):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks Student B the question, like the person on the card. Student C does not look!

2. Student B tells Student C: “Grandma asked Ben where he lived.” or “Mom asked Lily if she was hungry.”

3. Student C answers the question, like the person on the card. Then change roles.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask a new partner three questions: two Wh- questions and one yes / no question. Write the answers.

Then tell the class: “I asked Ana where she lived. I asked her if she had a pet. She said she had a cat.”
