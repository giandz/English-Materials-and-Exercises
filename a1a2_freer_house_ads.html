House Ads

Read three ads for houses. Compare the houses and their neighborhoods: bigger · cheaper · the safest · the most expensive…

How to play (with a partner)
  1. Read the three ads: the text, the house, the map 📍 and the colors. Look inside the houses with ✂️ and ▼ ▲.
  2. Roll 🎲. Answer the question, or talk about it with your partner.
  3. 👥 cards have an answer. Check it!
  4. Want new houses? Press 🔄 New ads.
You can say
A: Which house is better for a family?
B: House B. It’s bigger than house A, and the neighborhood is safer.
A: Yes, but it’s more expensive. House C is the cheapest.
B: That’s true, but it’s the farthest from the city center!
