What Did They Say?

Someone said something to you. Tell your partner what they said.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the person’s words in their head. Then A tells B: “My teacher said the test was the next day.”

2. Student B reacts: “Oh no! Are you ready?”

3. Press 👀 to check. Remember: one step back in time (is → was, will → would). Then change: B rolls, A reacts.
