Stage 3/5 · Then: Clarification

Meaning, form and pronunciation of the adverbs of sequence you just met.

1

Meaning

Adverbs of sequence show the order of steps in a recipe.

First,→ Next, / Then,→ After that,→ Finally,
First, peel the potatoes. Next, chop the onion. Then, fry the onion. After that, add the potatoes and water. Finally, simmer the soup for 20 minutes.
Concept check 1: "Finally" is the word we use for the first step.
Concept check 2: Can a recipe have more than one "Then" step?
Concept check 3: Does the order of the steps matter in a recipe?
2

Form

Adverb of sequence + comma + imperative verb.

First, peel the potatoes.
Next, chop the onion.
After that, add the water.
Finally, simmer the soup.

Notice: the adverb starts the sentence, a comma follows, then the base form of the verb (the imperative) — no subject, no "-s", no "to".

3

Pronunciation

Listen to your teacher and notice where the stress falls.

FIRST
NEXT
THEN
AFter that → AF-ter that
FI-nal-ly

"After that" and "Finally" have more than one syllable — the stress is on the first syllable of each. All five adverbs are followed by a short pause (the comma) before the next word.
