Which One Do You Want?

Talk in a shop and in your classroom: this one · that one · these ones · those ones · the red one

How to play (with a partner)
  1. Roll 🎲. Look at the card.
  2. 🛍️ card: Student A is 🧍 the customer. Student B works in the shop. Talk about the things here and there.
  3. 🏫 card: talk about real things in the room.
  4. Change roles. Roll again!
You can say
A: I need a scarf. How much is this one?
B: The blue one? It’s 20 dollars.
A: And that one?
B: The red one is 8 dollars.
A: This one is too expensive. I want the red one, please.
Talk about it
