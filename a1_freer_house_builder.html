🧑 People

Pick someone, then tap the house. On the floor they stand; on a chair, an armchair, a loveseat or a couch they sit; in a bed they sleep (two can share a double bed, one in each bunk); in the swimming pool they swim. With 📍 Relocate on, each person is in the house only once: putting them somewhere new takes them away from where they were (L toggles it). Tap a person to turn them, or to move them to the next seat or bunk. Drag a person to move them. 🧹 Erase takes them away. Press R to turn someone before you put them down.

With 🚶 Walk on (G), tap someone, then tap where they should go — or drag them there. They find their way round walls and furniture and through doors, get up from a seat or out of bed, and sit down, go to bed or get into the pool when they arrive. 🏃 Pace: run makes them run.

❓ How to use

  • Pick a tool in the toolbar at the bottom, or press 1–5 (and 6 for Options). ↩️ ↪️ undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y).
  • 🧱 Wall: drag along the grid lines to draw a wall. Tap a line to add one piece.
  • 🚪 Door: tap a line to add a door. Tap the door again to change which way it opens. After the fourth way, the next tap closes it, and the one after opens it again.
  • 🪟 Window: tap a line to add a window, or drag to add a row of windows.
  • 🪜 Straight, quarter-turn and half-turn staircases: tap a square to add stairs up to the next floor, starting there. A straight staircase is 3 squares long; a quarter-turn one is an L (two squares, a landing, two squares turning); a half-turn one is a U, 2 squares wide. Tap a staircase to turn it (turning ones also flip left/right), or press R before you tap. Every staircase has handrails on its open sides. With 🎨 Style, a staircase can be solid or cantilever (floating treads), and small things can then go underneath it.
  • 🚧 Fence: drag along the grid lines for a fence or handrail. A space closed in by fences becomes a green garden on the ground floor, or a balcony on an upper floor.
  • Gate: tap a fence to turn that piece into a gate of the same style (or tap an empty line). Tap a gate to open it, again to change which way it swings, and after the fourth way it closes. Fences also come in tall versions (1.8 m) of full board, lattice and chain-link — pick them with 🎨 Style.
  • 🪵 Column: tap a grid corner or the middle of a square to add a column. It is twice as thick as a wall.
  • 🏠 Roof (box gable, open gable, box shed, open shed or flat): drag from one corner of the roof to the opposite corner. Corners snap to grid corners and the middles of squares, so a roof can hang half a square past the walls. Tap a roof to turn it. The roof sits on top of the walls of the floor you are on.
  • 🛋️ Furniture: pick a 🍽️ dining table, 🪑 chair, 🛏️ single bed, double bed or bunk bed, side table, coffee table, dresser, wardrobe, armchair, loveseat or couch (and more) and tap a grid corner or the middle of a square. Tap it again to turn it, or press R before you tap. Drag it to move it.
  • 📚 Bookshelf, wall shelf, countertop, wall cabinet: furniture with levels for appliances. A wall cabinet hangs like a shelf and shares the countertop's own cabinet-door look, for matching upper and lower kitchen cabinets. A countertop is built like a wall: drag along the grid lines, on the side you want, and drag on from its end to make it longer. A bookshelf has three levels (two inside and the top). A wall shelf hangs on a wall: tap the wall on the side you want.
  • 🚿 Bathroom: a 🚽 toilet, a washbasin and a 🛁 bathtub stand on the floor like furniture, and a 🪞 mirror cabinet hangs on a wall. A 🚿 shower (a hot and a cold valve and the shower head) hangs on a wall, like a shelf: tap the wall on the side you want. It can go over a bathtub. A window or split air conditioner, an exhaust fan and a heating radiator mount on a wall the same way, from the Appliances row.
  • 📺 Appliances (television, monitor, game console, stereo, laptop, computer case, a table lamp and a stand lamp, microwave, an oven, a toaster, a blender, stove, kitchen sink, a refrigerator, a chest freezer, a washing machine, a clothes dryer, an over-under washer-dryer, and a tankless water heater — the stove and sink only go on a countertop, and the oven and the other big appliances only go on the floor). A window or split air conditioner (evaporator and condenser), an exhaust fan, a heating radiator and a tankless water heater all mount on a wall, like the shower. A chair, office chair, armchair, loveseat or couch can hold one appliance on its seat (a loveseat or couch also takes two side by side), and something small (about the size of a laptop) can go under a table or an ordinary chair.
  • 🪴 Objects (a rug, a planter, a book, four standing books): a rug lies on the floor, under furniture; the others go anywhere an appliance can — on the floor, on furniture, on a seat or under a table or chair — to show things like “the book is under the table”. In the 3D view, point at the floor under the table; on the plan, hold Shift while you tap the table to put it underneath. tap the floor, or a level of a table, bookshelf, shelf or countertop. Each level takes one appliance in the middle or two side by side, if they fit. A television only goes on the floor or on top of a piece. Tap an appliance to turn it, drag it to move it.
  • 🏊 Swimming pool: on the ground floor, drag across the squares where the pool should go. Pools are dug into the ground, so nothing can be built under them in the basement, and nothing can stand in the water.
  • Moving things: with 🏗️ Build, drag a column or a piece of furniture to move it, or drag a door or window along its wall. With 🏠 Roof picked, drag a roof's corner handles to resize it.
  • 🎨 Style: tap a wall, door, window, fence, column, roof or floor (a floor picks the whole room). Drag a box to pick many walls, fences or floor squares at once: start the drag on a wall to pick walls, on a fence to pick fences, or anywhere else to pick floor. It lights up, and the drawer shows the styles for it. Walls can be styled on one side or both. Roofs can be flat, box gable, open gable, box shed or open shed, in clay tile, concrete or corrugated metal. Open roofs carry the outside walls up to meet the roof. Floors can be hardwood, ceramic tile or carpet, walls can be tiled for a bathroom or kitchen, or papered inside with striped, floral, polka dot, trellis or gingham wallpaper — each also half-panelled, with painted wood panelling and a chair rail under the paper. Apply to all gives the same style to every one in the house.
  • 🧑 People: put people in the house. They come from the characters folder next to this page (characters saved from the Character Poser); you can also open a folder or some character files with the buttons in the drawer. Pick someone and tap: on the floor they stand, on a chair, armchair, loveseat or couch they sit, in a bed they sleep (two can share a double bed, one in each bunk of a bunk bed), and in the swimming pool they swim, with the water up to their shoulders. With 📍 Relocate on (or press L), each character is in the house only once: putting them down somewhere takes away any copy of them already there, on any floor. Tap a person to turn them (or move them to the next seat or bunk), drag to move them, and press R to turn someone before you put them down. People move with the seat or bed they are on, stand up if it is taken away, and are saved with the house. With 🚶 Walk on (or press G), tap someone and then tap where they should go (or drag them there): they walk there, round the walls and furniture and through the doors, standing up first if they were sitting, and sitting down, going to bed or getting into the pool when they arrive. 🏃 Pace: run in the drawer makes them run. The walk is played with the animations in the animations folder (made with the Character Keyframes editor).
  • 🧹 Erase: tap a wall, door, window, fence, column, stairs or a person to remove it, or drag along a line to remove many.
  • 🔼 🔽: go up or down a floor, from the basement (B1F) to the second floor (2F). You can also press PgUp and PgDn. Floors above the one you are on are hidden.
  • 👁️ View: show the 3D view, the floor plan, or both. ⟲ ⟳ turn the house (Q, E). 🧱 lowers all the walls so you can see inside (W), and ✂️ Cutout keeps the walls at the back full height and cuts the rest down (X). In both of these modes a slider sets how much of the walls to keep, from almost nothing to the full height (or press [ for less and ] for more); the two modes share it. Floors (F) and Ceiling (C) show or hide the floors between storeys, and Roofs (T) shows or hides the roofs.
  • Zoom and pan: each view has − 100% + buttons in its corner (100% puts it back). With a mouse, the wheel zooms where you point, and a right-drag, middle-drag or Space + drag moves the picture; on a trackpad, pinch to zoom and scroll to move. On a touch screen, pinch and drag with two fingers. +, − and 0 work on the keyboard too.
  • Closed rooms get a floor, and the plan shows their size. One square is 1 metre.
  • ⚙️ Options: change the grid size, save your house to a file or open a saved one, or clear everything.
