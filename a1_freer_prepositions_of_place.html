How to play (with a partner)
  1. Student A looks at the house. Student B doesn't look.
  2. Student B rolls 🎲 and asks the question. Student A answers.
  3. Look at the house together. Is the answer correct?
  4. Change roles. Make a new house 🏠 and play again!
