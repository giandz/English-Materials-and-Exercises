Modal Verbs Lab: can, could, may, might, should, must

I can swim. · Could you help me? · May I come in? · It might rain. · You should rest. · You mustn’t run.

The pattern
subject+ modal+ base verb…
modal+ subject+ base verb…?
canability · request · permission
couldpast ability · polite request · possibility
mayformal permission · possibility
mightpossibility
shouldadvice
mustrules · necessity

Same form for every subject: I can, she can — no -s.

No “to” after the modal: I can swim (not can to swim).

No do / does: Can you…? · I can’t… (not Do you can…? · I don’t can…).

Negatives: can’t, couldn’t, shouldn’t, mustn’t, may not, might not.
