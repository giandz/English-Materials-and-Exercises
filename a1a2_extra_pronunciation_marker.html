Pronunciation Marker

Type or paste English. The stress, connected speech and intonation marks appear as you type.

Try:
Marked text

Tap a word to give it the main stress. This adds *stars* round it in your text. Tap again to take it away.

The marks come from spelling rules and a short word list, so check them. Some words will be wrong.

Correct a word's stress

One word per line. Split it into syllables with hyphens and write the stressed syllable in capitals: PHO-to-graph, pho-TOG-ra-phy. Put + before a secondary stress: +in-tro-DUCE. This device remembers your corrections.
