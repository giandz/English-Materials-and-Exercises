When Is It?

Ask and answer about dates — and say numbers.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

📅 Ask: A asks the question. B answers with in, on or at.

🔢 Say it: open 🤫 (only A looks!). A says the number or the date. B writes it. Then look together: is it right?

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner. “When is your birthday? What is your favorite month? Why?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana’s birthday is on May 5th. Her favorite month is December.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
