Be-Verb Lab: Wh-questions with am / is / are

What is this? · Who is she? · Where are you from? · How old are you?

The pattern
Wh-word+ am / is / are+ subject+ … ?
amIfirst person singular
ishe, she, itthird person singular
areyou, we, theysecond person & plural

With the verb be, the verb itself moves before the subject to form questions — no separate auxiliary is needed. The main verb is the auxiliary.

am + not: I’m not (we don’t use “amn’t”).
