World Quiz

Ask and answer about food, languages, money and celebrations around the world.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and makes the question: Where / avocados / grow? → “Where are avocados grown?”

2. Student B chooses an answer and says the full sentence: “I think they’re grown in Mexico.”

3. Press 👀 to check. A correct answer = 1 point. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Talk about your country (or a country you know well). Ask your partner:

“What food is grown or made in your country?” · “What languages are spoken there?”

“What money is used there? What money was used before?” · “What festivals are celebrated? What is eaten?”

Example: “Arepas are made in Venezuela. They’re made with corn. The bolívar is used there. Carnival is celebrated in February or March.”
