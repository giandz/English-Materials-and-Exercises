The Dream Machine

Imagine, dream — and give advice.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

💭 Dream: answer the question with two or three ideas. “If I could fly, I’d visit my family every weekend. I might fly to Japan, too!” B asks one more question: “Would you take your dog?”

💡 Advice: B reads the problem as if it were their own. A gives advice: “If I were you, I’d talk to your neighbor.” B answers: “Good idea!” or “Hmm, I don’t think that would work, because…”

2. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
