My Profile

Ask and answer questions for a profile on an app for meeting new people.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲, reads what to ask about and makes the question: 💪 something they do well → “What are you good at?”

Need help? Press 🧩 for a clue: the words that go together (good at).

2. Student B answers with a full sentence and one more detail: “I’m good at cooking. I make great pizza!”

3. Press 👀 to check the question. Was it the same? Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Write your partner’s profile. Write their name, then write their answers. Then read it to them: “Your name is Ana. You’re good at…” Is it correct?
