My Profile

Ask and answer questions for a profile on an app for meeting new people.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and makes the question: What / you / good at? → “What are you good at?”

2. Student B answers with a full sentence and one more detail: “I’m good at cooking. I make great pizza!”

3. Press 👀 to check the question. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
