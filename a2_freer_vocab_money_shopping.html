At the Mall

Buy and sell things — and talk about what they are made of.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🛍️ Role-play: A is the customer, B is the sales assistant. Ask about the thing: the price, the material, the size. Then pay.

🔎 What is it? Open 🤫 (only A looks!). A describes the thing: “It’s made of… You wear it on… It costs about…” B guesses.

2. Press 👀 for useful phrases. Then change roles.
