Calendar Talk

When do you do things? Use in, on or at.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Say in, on or at and a true sentence about you: “On Saturday mornings, I go to the market.”

2. Student B asks one more question: “What do you buy?”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A asks.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about their year. “When is your birthday? What time do you get up? When is your vacation?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana’s birthday is on May 5th. She gets up at six.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
