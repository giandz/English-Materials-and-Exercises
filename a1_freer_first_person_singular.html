Me Too!

Talk about your day. Is your partner’s day the same?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Finish the sentence. It is about you! “I wake up at seven.”

2. Student B answers: “Me too!” or “Not me. I wake up at eight.”

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
