Let’s Go!

Make plans for the weekend — and talk about what you enjoy doing.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

📍 Invite: A invites B to the place on the card: “Do you enjoy…? How about going to…?” B answers: “I love… / I don’t mind… / I hate…” Then agree on a plan!

💬 Talk: answer the question. Then ask your partner.

2. Press 👀 for useful phrases. Then change: B rolls.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about free time. “What do you enjoy doing on weekends? What do you hate doing? What are you good at?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana enjoys going to the theater. She hates waiting in lines.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
