N-400: Trips Outside the U.S. — Role Play

Work with a partner. One person is the officer. One person is the applicant. Then change!

The officer asks
How many trips have you taken in the last five years? Where did you go? When did you leave? When did you come back? How long were you there? Why did you go? Who did you go with? Did you take any trips of six months or more?
1 · Role cards

The applicant rolls the dice and gets a card with their trips. Don’t show it to the officer! The officer asks the questions above and writes the answers. Then compare.

Press the dice to get your trips.
Cards: 0
2 · Follow-up questions

Roll the dice. Your partner hears the question and answers about a trip on their card. Then pass the dice.

Press the dice to start.
Questions: 0
3 · A trip you took

Ask your partners about a real trip they took (in the U.S. or to another country). Write their answers.
