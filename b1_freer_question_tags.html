I Think I Know You…

Guess things about your partner — and check with a question tag.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Finish the sentence with a guess about Student B, and add the tag: “You like spicy food, don’t you?”

2. Sure? Use a ↘ falling voice. Not sure? Use a ↗ rising voice.

3. B answers: “Yes, I do!” / “No, I don’t, actually.” A gets a point for every correct guess. Press 👀 to check the tag.

4. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
