Pass It On!

Tell your partner what people said.

How to play (groups of 3):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the words to Student B. Student C does not look!

2. Student B tells Student C: “Ben said he was hungry.”

3. Student C answers the question on the card. Then change roles.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner three questions: Are you hungry? · Do you have a pet? · What are you reading now?

Then tell the class: “Ana told me she was hungry. She said she had a cat.”
