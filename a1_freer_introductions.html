A short speaking activity

🎭 Role play
Work in pairs. Choose a scene and act it out. Then switch roles.
😊 Scene A — informal
Student A
It's your first day at a new school. Introduce yourself to a classmate.
  • Hi! I'm…
  • What's your name?
  • Where are you from?
Student B
You already go to this school. Introduce your friend to the new student too.
  • Nice to meet you!
  • This is my friend, …
  • Welcome to the class!
🤝 Scene B — formal
New employee
It's your first day at a new job. Introduce yourself to your manager.
  • My name is…
  • It's a pleasure to meet you.
  • I'm very happy to be here.
Manager
Welcome the new employee, then introduce them to a colleague.
  • Good morning. I'm…
  • Let me introduce you to…
  • How do you do?
