Talking about finished actions on vacation

The idea: we use the past simple for actions that are finished. They happened at a time in the past — last summer, yesterday, on Saturday.

Common mistakes
✗I goed to the beach.
✓I went to the beach.
✗I didn't bought souvenirs.
✓I didn't buy souvenirs.
✗Did you took photos?
✓Did you take photos?
✗We visit a museum last summer.
✓We visited a museum last summer.
