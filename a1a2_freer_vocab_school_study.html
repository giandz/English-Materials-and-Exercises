Teacher Says

Give instructions like a teacher — and talk about how you study.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

👩‍🏫 Teacher says: A is the teacher. A reads the instruction. B does it (or mimes it)! If the card says Don’t, B does nothing.

💬 Talk: answer the question. Then ask your partner.

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B is the teacher.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about studying. “How do you learn new words? Do you use apps or paper? What was your favorite subject at school?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana learns new words with flashcards. Her favorite subject was art.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
