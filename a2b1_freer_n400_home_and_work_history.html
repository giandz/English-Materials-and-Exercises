N-400: Home and Work History — Role Play

Work with a partner. One person is the officer. One person is the applicant. Then change!

You can say
I’ve lived there since … I’ve worked there for … Before that, I … I lived / worked there from … to … I left that job because … I was laid off. / I quit. Between … and …, I wasn’t working. I was …
1 · Role cards

The applicant rolls the dice and gets a card with a five-year history. Don’t show it to the officer! The officer asks about every home and every job, and writes the answers. Then compare: are there any missing months?

Press the dice to get your history.

2 · The officer’s questions
