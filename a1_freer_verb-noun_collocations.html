Which Verb?

Find the right verb. Then ask your partner a question.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Choose the right verb and ask: “How often do you take a photo?”

2. Student B answers. It’s true! “I take photos every day!”

3. Press 👀 to check the verb. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
