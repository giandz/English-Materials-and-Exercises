Regular participles: + ed — visited, called, worked, tried.

Irregular participles: be → been · go → gone / been · eat → eaten · see → seen · do → done · buy → bought · write → written · meet → met · win → won · ride → ridden · fly → flown · swim → swum · break → broken · know → known · have → had · leave → left · make → made · send → sent

Finished time (yesterday, last year, in 2019, two days ago) → past simple, not present perfect.
