Love It, Hate It, Want It

Talk about your hobbies, your feelings and your plans.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and finishes the sentence about the topic on the card. It’s true! “About food: I can’t stand eating fish.”

2. Student B asks one more question: “Why not? Did you try sushi?”

3. Press 👀 to check the pattern. Then change: B rolls, A asks.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about their hobbies and plans. “What do you enjoy doing? What can’t you stand? What do you want to do this year?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana enjoys dancing salsa. She wants to learn French this year.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
