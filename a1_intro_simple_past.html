How was your vacation?

A simple past conversation about a fun vacation

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

🌴 Mia and Ben returned from the beach
subjectregular past verbactivityquestion
👩

Mia

Hi, Ben! Did you enjoy your vacation?
👨

Ben

Yes, I did. I relaxed at the beach and walked near the water.
👩

Mia

Great! What did you do on Monday?
👨

Ben

I visited a small museum. I listened to music there.
👩

Mia

Did you play any sports?
👨

Ben

Yes. I played volleyball with my friends. We watched the sunset too.
👩

Mia

That sounds fun! Did you cook dinner?
👨

Ben

No, I didn’t. We ordered pizza and talked at a café.
👩

Mia

What did you do on your last day?
👨

Ben

I cleaned my room, packed my bags, and thanked my host.
👩

Mia

Wonderful. I hope you enjoyed your trip!
