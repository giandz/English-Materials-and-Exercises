Tech Help Desk

A customer has a problem. The tech support answers with a flowchart only — you say it in English!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Roll 🎲. Student B is the customer 🙋: read the problem in the speech bubble.

2. Student A is tech support 🧑‍🔧. Tech support only “speaks” in flowcharts! Say the flowchart in English — both paths: “If you restart it, it works again. If you don’t restart it, it stays frozen.”

3. B answers: “OK, thanks!” or asks one more question: “And what happens if…?”

4. Press 👀 to check your sentences. Then change: B is tech support, A is the customer.
