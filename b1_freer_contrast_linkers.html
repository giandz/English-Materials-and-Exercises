Pros and Cons

Every decision has a good side and a bad side. Talk about both!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and reads the decision and the linker.

2. A says one pro 👍 and one con 👎 in one sentence, with the linker: “Although a big city is exciting, it’s very expensive.”

3. Student B answers with a different linker: “True. However, there are more jobs!”

4. Press 👀 for ideas. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
