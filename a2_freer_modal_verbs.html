What Would You Say?

Read the situation. Say the right thing with a modal verb.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Read the situation and the job (advice, a polite request, permission…). Say it with a modal verb: “You should take a pill.”

2. Student B answers or reacts: “Good idea, thanks!” / “Sure, no problem.”

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A reacts.
