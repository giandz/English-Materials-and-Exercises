Celebrations: What Are You Going to Do?

Two short conversations about celebration plans, using the "going to" future.

●  affirmative ●  negative ●  question ●  celebration vocabulary
Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

Conversation 1 — Birthday
🎂 Ana and Leo, talking about Leo's birthday
👩
Ana
What are you going to do for your birthday, Leo?
🧑
Leo
I am going to have a party with my friends! 🎉
👩
Ana
Are you going to invite your cousins too?
🧑
Leo
Yes, I am. I am going to invite my whole family.
👩
Ana
Is your mom going to bake a cake? 🎂
🧑
Leo
Yes, she is going to bake a chocolate cake, and I am going to blow out the candles! 🕯️
👩
Ana
Nice! And are you going to open your presents too? 🎁
🧑
Leo
Of course! But I am not going to open them before the party — I am going to wait for everyone.
Conversation 2 — New Year
🎆 Priya and Sam, talking about New Year's Eve
👩
Priya
What are you going to do on New Year's Eve, Sam?
🧑
Sam
I am going to watch the fireworks with my family. 🎆
👩
Priya
Are you going to make any resolutions this year?
🧑
Sam
Yes, I am going to exercise more. 💪 What about your family?
👩
Priya
We are going to have a big family dinner and are going to eat a special meal at midnight. 🍽️
🧑
Sam
That sounds fun! I am not going to stay up until midnight — I's too tired!
👩
Priya
Really? I am going to count down with everyone at midnight! 🎊
🧑
Sam
Have fun! I am going to be asleep by then. 😴

Quick reference
Use am / is / are + going to + verb for plans (affirmative), am / is / are + not + going to + verb to say something isn't planned (negative), and am / is / are + subject + going to + verb to ask about someone's plans (question). Words in purple are celebration vocabulary — the things people do during a festivity.
