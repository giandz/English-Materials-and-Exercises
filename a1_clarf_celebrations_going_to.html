Celebrations: Going To — Clarification

How to talk about your plans for a celebration.

We use going to for plans we have already decided. The form is am / is / are + going to + verb.
The three forms
affirmativenegativequestion
affirmative

I am going to have a party.

She is going to bake a cake.

We are going to watch the fireworks.

negative

I am not going to open my presents.

He is not going to stay up until midnight.

question

Are you going to invite your cousins?

What are you going to do on New Year's Eve?

In questions, am / is / are moves in front of the subject: You are going to… → Are you going to…?
When does it happen?
A plan made before now, for a moment after now. That is why we say going to and not just the present.
Check the meaning
Answer the questions about each sentence. These check the meaning, not the form.
Short answers

Are you going to invite your family? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Is your mom going to bake a cake? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
