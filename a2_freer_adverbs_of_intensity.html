Too Hot, Very Nice

How strong is it? And is it a problem?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

💬 Describe: choose a word from the scale and give your opinion. “My city is quite expensive.” B agrees or not: “Really? I think it’s extremely expensive!”

🤔 Problem: say why. Use too or not … enough. “I can’t drink it. It’s too hot.”

2. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about their city and home. “What’s your city like? Is it expensive? What’s your home like? Is there a problem with it?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana’s city is extremely lively, but it’s a bit dangerous. Her apartment isn’t big enough.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
