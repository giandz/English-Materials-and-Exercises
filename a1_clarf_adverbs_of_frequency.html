Adverbs of Frequency

How often something happens, from 100% of the time to 0%.

always almost always often / usually sometimes hardly ever / rarely almost never never
Word order: adverbs of frequency go before the main verb (She often cooks), but after the verb "be" (She is often tired) and after auxiliary verbs (She has always liked coffee).
The seven adverbs
100%
always

I always brush my teeth before bed.

95%
almost always

He's almost always the first one in the office.

75%
often / usually

We usually eat dinner together.

50%
sometimes

Sometimes I take the bus instead of driving.

25%
hardly ever / rarely

She rarely stays up past midnight.

5%
almost never

They almost never talk about money.

0%
never

I never eat breakfast before 8am.

There it is: the adverb goes before an ordinary verb, but after be, and after the auxiliary in negatives and questions. One adverb, three different slots — and it is always next to the verb, never between the verb and its object.
⚠️ One exception worth knowing: sometimes, usually and often can also start the sentence — Sometimes I take the bus. always and never cannot: Always I drink coffee.
Common mistakes
✗She cooks often dinner.
✓She often cooks dinner.
✗We eat usually at seven.
✓We usually eat at seven.
✗He never is late.
✓He is never late.
✗Always I drink coffee in the morning.
✓I always drink coffee in the morning.
✗I don't never eat meat.
✓I never eat meat. / I don't ever eat meat.
✗How often you go to the gym?
✓How often do you go to the gym?
✗My brother never eat breakfast.
✓My brother never eats breakfast.
