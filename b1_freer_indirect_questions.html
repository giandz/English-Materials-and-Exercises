The Information Desk

One of you needs help. The other works at the information desk. Ask politely!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. A is the visitor: make the question on the card polite: “Excuse me, do you know where the nearest pharmacy is?”

2. Student B works at the desk and answers — invent the information! “Yes, it’s on Main Street, next to the bank.”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls and becomes the visitor.
