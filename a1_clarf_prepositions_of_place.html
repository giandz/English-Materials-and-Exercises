Where is the lamp? → It's on the side table.

Where are the books? → They're under the table.

The preposition goes before the place: on the table, in the bookcase.

Some have two or three words: next to the sofa, in front of the sofa.

between has two places: between the sofa and the armchair.

Common mistakes
✗The lamp is next the sofa.
✓The lamp is next to the sofa.
✗The bookcase is behind of the sofa.
✓The bookcase is behind the sofa.
✗The rug is in front the sofa.
✓The rug is in front of the sofa.
✗The books are under of the table.
✓The books are under the table.
✗The lamp is in the table.
✓The lamp is on the table.
✗The lamp is on table.
✓The lamp is on the table.
✗Where the books are?
✓Where are the books?
✗The table is between the sofa or the armchair.
✓The table is between the sofa and the armchair.
