Tell Me About Them

Talk about the people in your life.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Finish the sentence. It’s true! “My mom gets up at six.” / “My cat doesn’t drive!”

2. Student B asks one more question: “Does she drink coffee?”

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A asks.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner about one person in their family. “Who is he? What does he do? Where does he live? What does he like?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana’s brother is Luis. He works in a bank.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
