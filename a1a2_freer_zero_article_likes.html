This or That?

Talk about what you like, and why.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Roll 🎲. Look at the two things on the card.

2. Say which one you prefer, and why — in general: “I prefer cats. Cats are quiet. I don’t mind dogs.”

3. Your partner answers: “Really? I can’t stand cats! I love dogs. Dogs are friendly.”

4. Press 👀 to check. Then roll again.
