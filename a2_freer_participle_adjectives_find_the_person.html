How to play

  1. Read the description. Before you start, you can open the picture of the person.
  2. Press 🎬 Start. The picture closes and the clock starts.
  3. Click the person. A wrong click costs 5 seconds.
  4. Need help? 🔍 Magnify shows a bigger view under the pointer. 🐣 Easier turns part of the picture grey: the person isn’t there. You can use it three times.

Score

  • Up to 1000 points, for the time you have left.
  • 🔍 Magnifying glass: ×0.85
  • 🐣 Easier: ×0.8 each time
  • 👤 Opening the picture after you start: ×0.6
