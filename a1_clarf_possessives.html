Possessives — Quick Reference

Saying who something belongs to

The idea: possessives show who something belongs to. We use a special word before the noun (my hat) or add 's to a name or person (Leo's hat).

Possessive 's

Singular noun or name: add 's.
Ana's book  ·  the dog's bone  ·  my brother's hat

Plural noun ending in -s: add only '.
the students' books  ·  my parents' house

Plural noun NOT ending in -s: add 's, like a singular noun.
the children's toys  ·  the men's shoes

Whose…?

We ask about possession with Whose + noun.
Whose shoes are these? — They're my shoes. / They're Leo's.
