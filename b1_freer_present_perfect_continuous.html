Detective Questions

Look at the clues. What has your partner been doing? And how long?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🔍 Clue: B reads the clue and acts it out. A is the detective: “Have you been crying?” B: “No, I haven’t. I’ve been cutting onions!”

⏳ How long: A asks the question. B answers with for or since and one more detail.

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
