Regret Rewind

Something went wrong. Rewind the past — then blame someone, or make them feel better!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Read what happened (📍) and say your regret: “If I had set an alarm, I would have caught the flight.”

2. Student B chooses: 😠 criticize — “Well, if you hadn’t stayed up so late…” — or 🤗 comfort — “Don’t worry. If you had caught it, you might have sat next to a crying baby!”

3. Press 👀 to see the forms. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
