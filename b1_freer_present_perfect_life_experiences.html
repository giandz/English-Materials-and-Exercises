Have You Ever…?

Find out about your partner’s life. Then get the full story.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks: “Have you ever ridden a horse?”

2. If B says “No, I’ve never ridden a horse”, A asks: “Would you like to?” If B says “Yes, I have”, A asks two follow-up questions in the past simple, using the question words on the card: “When did you ride one? How was it?”

3. Press 👀 to check the forms. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Interview your partner about their life story. “Which countries have you been to? What’s the strangest food you’ve ever eaten? What have you never done, but would love to do?” Write the answers — and one detail for each.

Then tell someone else: “Ana has been to four countries. She went to Peru in 2019 and loved it.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
