Not This Time!

You usually do the same thing — but your plan is different this time!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Say the sentence: “I usually clean my room on Saturdays, but this Saturday I’m going to go to the beach.”

2. Student B asks one question about the plan: “Who are you going to go with?”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A asks.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about the weekend. “What do you usually do on Saturdays? What are you going to do this Saturday?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana usually works on Saturdays, but this Saturday she’s going to relax.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
