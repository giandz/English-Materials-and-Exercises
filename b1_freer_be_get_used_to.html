A New Life

Big changes! What would be hard to get used to — and what are you already used to?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🔄 A big change: imagine it. Say two things you’d have to get used to and one thing you’re already used to: “I’d have to get used to sleeping in the day. But I’m used to working late!”

⏪ Then and now: compare your life before and now: “I used to walk to school. Now I’m used to driving everywhere.”

2. Student B asks one more question. Press 👀 for help. Then change.
