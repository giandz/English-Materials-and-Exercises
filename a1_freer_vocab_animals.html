What Animal Is It?

Guess the animal — and talk about animals you like.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🤫 Guess: only A looks at the animal. B asks yes / no questions: “Can it fly? Has it got four legs? Does it live on a farm?” A answers: “Yes, it can. / No, it hasn’t.” Can B guess in 10 questions?

💬 Talk: answer the question. Then ask your partner.

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Ask your partner. “What’s your favorite animal? What can it do? Which animal are you scared of?” Write the answers.

Then say: “Ana’s favorite animal is the dolphin. Dolphins can swim very fast.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
