How Often?

Talk about your life. How often do you do these things?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Say a true sentence about you. Use always, usually, often, sometimes, hardly ever or never. “I sometimes cook dinner.”

2. Student B asks one more question: “Really? What do you cook?”

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
