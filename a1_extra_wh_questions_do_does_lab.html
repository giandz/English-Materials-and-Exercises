Wh-Words Lab: Wh-questions with do / does

Where does she live? · How often do you practice? · What time does the train leave?

The pattern
Wh-word+ do / does+ subject+ base verb+ … ?
doI, you, we, theyfirst/second person & plural
doeshe, she, itthird person singular

In negatives and questions, do/does carries the tense — the main verb stays in its base form.
