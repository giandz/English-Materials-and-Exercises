Spelling — Quick Reference

Asking for, giving, and correcting spelling

The idea: we spell a word by saying its letters one by one. This is very useful for names, emails, and other personal information.
Asking for spelling

How do you spell your name?  ·  Could you spell that, please?  ·  Is that spelled K-A-T-E?

Giving and correcting spelling

It's spelled C-A-T-E.  ·  It's Cate with a C.

To correct someone: No, it's [word] with a [letter]. — "No, it's Cate with a C."
