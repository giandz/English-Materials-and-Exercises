Let's Talk: How Much? How Many?

A short speaking activity about quantities

🗣️ Mingle — ask and answer
Talk to three classmates. Ask 2–3 questions from the cards. Tap a card after you ask it.

How much time do you spend on your phone every day?

✓ Asked

✓ Asked
🧊 Describe your fridge
Imagine your fridge or kitchen at home right now. Tell your partner what you have and don't have, using: a lot of, many, much, a few, a little, some, any, most, all, none.

e.g. "I have a lot of vegetables, but I don't have much fruit. I have a few eggs, but I don't have any milk!"
