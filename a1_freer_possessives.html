Let's Talk: Whose Is It?

A short speaking activity about possessions and clothes

🗣️ Talk about your things
Talk to a partner. Ask 3 questions from the cards. Tap a card after you ask it.
1
What is your favorite piece of clothing?
✓ Asked
2
Whose clothes do you sometimes borrow?
✓ Asked
3
What's in your bag right now?
✓ Asked
4
What color are your shoes today?
✓ Asked
5
Do you have a pet? What is its name?
✓ Asked
6
Whose style do you like — a friend's or a family member's?
✓ Asked
👀 Describe your partner
Work in pairs. Look at your partner and describe three things they are wearing. Then switch roles.
Student A
Describe three things your partner is wearing, using "your".
  • I like your …
  • Is that your …?
  • Your … is/are very nice.
Student B
Answer, and say who something belongs to if it isn't yours.
  • Thanks! It's mine.
  • No, it's not mine — it's my …'s.
  • Yes, these are my …
