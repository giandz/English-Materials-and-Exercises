Our teachers

Fabio and Martin talk about their teachers.

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

🏫 classroom, before class
Conversation
👱
Fabio
Hi Martin! Who is your English teacher?
🧑‍🦱
Martin
My teacher is Mrs. Lopez. She teaches English and Spanish.
👱
Fabio
Nice! My teacher is Mr. Smith. He has a big office with many books.
🧑‍🦱
Martin
Wow! Mrs. Lopez has a small office. She goes to work by bus every day.
👱
Fabio
Mr. Smith drives to school. He lives near the school.
🧑‍🦱
Martin
Interesting. My sister studies here too. Her teacher is Ms. Baker.
👱
Fabio
Ms. Baker! She does art class on Fridays. My brother likes her class a lot.
🧑‍🦱
Martin
Nice! Ms. Baker also plays the piano. She has a piano in her classroom.
👱
Fabio
Cool! What time does your class start?
🧑‍🦱
Martin
It starts at 9. Mrs. Lopez always brings coffee to class!
👱
Fabio
Ha! Mr. Smith doesn't drink coffee. He drinks tea.
🧑‍🦱
Martin
Ha, funny! Okay, let's go. Class starts now.

Quick reference
With he/she, the verb gets an -s: teaches, has, goes, does, likes. We use Mr. for men, Mrs. for married women, and Ms. for women (any status) — always with a family name.
