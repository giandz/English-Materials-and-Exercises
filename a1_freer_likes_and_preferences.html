Love It or Hate It?

How much do you like it? Do you and your partner agree?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. Choose a face: ❤️ 🤩 👍 😐 👎 ☠️. Say your sentence: “I love rainy days.” / “I can’t stand rainy days.”

2. Student B answers: “Me too!” or “Really? I don’t mind them.”

3. Press 👀 to see examples. Then change: B rolls, A answers.
