Office News

You heard something at work. Tell your partner what people said, asked or told someone to do.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Roll 🎲. Read the words on the card aloud, like the person.

2. Your partner reports it: “Ms. Park told Dan not to forget the meeting.” · “Luis said he couldn’t work on Sunday.” · “Tom asked Rosa where the keys were.”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change roles.

Roll the dice to start.
Rolls: 0
Now you

Think about your work or school this week. What did your boss, your coworkers or your teacher say?

Tell your partner three things: one thing someone said, one thing someone asked you to do, and one question someone asked you.

“My boss said she would be on vacation next week. She asked me to answer her emails. A coworker asked me if I could work on Saturday.”

Your partner asks one more question about each: “What did you tell her?”
