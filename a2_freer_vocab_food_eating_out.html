The Busy Restaurant

Be the waiter and the customer — and talk about the food you love.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

🍽️ Role-play: A is the waiter, B is the customer. Use the restaurant and the customer card: book, order, ask about the food, pay and leave a tip.

😋 Describe a dish: A describes a dish from that place — sweet? salty? spicy? a starter or a main course? B guesses or asks questions.

2. Press 👀 for useful phrases. Then change roles.
