What’s in Your Kitchen?

Ask about your partner’s home. Answer with the truth!

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and asks the question: “Is there any milk in your fridge?” / “Are there any eggs?”

2. Student B answers. It’s true! “Yes, there is some milk.” / “No, there aren’t any eggs.”

3. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A answers.

Roll the dice to start.

Now you

Ask your partner about their home and city. “What’s in your fridge? Is there a park in your street? Are there any good restaurants in your city?” Write the answers.

Then say: “In Ana’s fridge, there is some cheese and there are some eggs.” Another partner? Press ➕ Add partner.
