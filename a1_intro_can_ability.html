Quick reference
Can / can't + verb talks about ability. Use it the same way for every person: I can, you can, he/she/it can, we can, they can — never "cans".
★★★★★I can … very well
★★★★☆I can … well
★★★☆☆I can … quite well
★★☆☆☆I can … a little
☆☆☆☆☆I can't … at all
