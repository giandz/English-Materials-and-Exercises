How often...? — Exact answers

A short conversation using exact frequency expressions like "once a week" and "every day".

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

☕ two coworkers, Monday morning, coffee break
Conversation
👨‍🦱
Sam
Morning, Ana! How often do you go to the gym?
🙋
Ana
I go three times a week — Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.
👨‍🦱
Sam
Wow, that's a lot! I only go once a week.
🙋
Ana
That's still good! Do you exercise on other days?
👨‍🦱
Sam
Not really. I go for a walk every day, though. It's easy and free.
🙋
Ana
Every day? That's great. I walk my dog once every two days.
👨‍🦱
Sam
How often do you see your family?
🙋
Ana
I call my parents every day, but I visit them once a month.
👨‍🦱
Sam
I visit mine twice a year. They live far away.
🙋
Ana
That must be hard. Do you call them often?
👨‍🦱
Sam
I call them once a week, usually on Sundays.
🙋
Ana
That's a nice routine!

Quick reference
Exact frequency expressions (like once a week or every day) usually go at the end of the sentence: I go to the gym three times a week. This is different from adverbs like usually or always, which go before the main verb.
