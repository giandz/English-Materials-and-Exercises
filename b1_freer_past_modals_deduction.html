What Happened Here?

You come home. Nobody is there, but there are clues. What happened while you were out?

How to play (pairs):

1. Roll 🎲. Look at the picture and read the clues together.

2. Talk about the past: what must have happened? What might have happened? What can’t have happened? “He must have left in a hurry. He might have gone to work.”

3. Agree on one story and say how sure you are (for example 75%: “He probably forgot his watch.”).

4. Press 👀 to see what really happened. Were you right? Then roll again.
