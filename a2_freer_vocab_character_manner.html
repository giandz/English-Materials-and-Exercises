People Watching

Talk about the people you know — and act out how people do things.

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲. There are two kinds of card:

👥 Who do you know? Talk about a real person: “My aunt is really generous. She always…” Student B asks one more question.

🎭 Act it out: open 🤫 (only A looks!) and do the action in that way. B guesses: “You’re ordering a coffee… rudely!”

2. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls, A listens or guesses.

Ask your partner about the people in their life. “What are you like? Who is the funniest person you know? What are you good at?” Write the answers.
