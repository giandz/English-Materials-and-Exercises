-ing = the thing or person that causes the feeling. The lecture was boring. · Long meetings are tiring. · He is an interesting person.

-ed = the person (or animal) who has the feeling. I was bored. · He is tired. · Rex is interested in his plate.

Ask: Does it make the feeling? → -ing. Does it have the feeling? → -ed.

Careful with people: She is boring = other people feel bored with her. She is bored = she feels bored.

-ed adjectives + preposition: interested in · excited about · bored with / by · tired of · surprised by · disappointed with / by · annoyed at / with · frightened of / by.

Spelling: -e verbs drop the e before -ing (excite → exciting, but excited) · -y after a consonant → -ied (satisfy → satisfied, but satisfying).
