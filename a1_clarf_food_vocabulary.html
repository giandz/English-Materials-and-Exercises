Food Vocabulary — Clarification

Ingredients, meals, and how to talk about what you eat.

Ingredients are the things you cook with. A meal is what you put on the plate and eat.
Ingredients by group
🥩 Meatsbeef · chicken · bacon · pork
🧀 Dairy & eggsmilk · cheese · butter · egg
🍎 Fruitsapple · banana · grapes · strawberry
🥕 Vegetablesbroccoli · carrot · corn · potato
The parts of a meal
🍲 Appetizerthe small first dish — soup, dumplings, falafel
🍔 Main coursethe big dish — hamburger, pizza, spaghetti, taco
🍟 Side disha small dish with the main — fries, rice, bread, salad
🍰 Dessertthe sweet dish at the end — cake, ice cream, cookie, pie
Some food you count, some you do not

I eat an apple every day. → count: one apple, two apples

I drink milk every day. → noncount: never two milks

Count: apple, banana, egg, carrot, potato, cookie

Noncount: milk, cheese, butter, rice, bread, corn

Talking about food

What is your favourite food?

I like spicy food, but I don't like fish.

Would you like a dessert?

Common mistakes
✗I eat a milk every morning.
✓I drink milk every morning.
✗Cake is a appetizer.
✓Cake is a dessert.
✗I want a apple.
✓I want an apple.
✗We had three breads.
✓We had three pieces of bread.
✗She like spicy food.
✓She likes spicy food.
