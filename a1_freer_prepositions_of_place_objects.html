Draw It!

Listen and draw. Is your picture right?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and opens 🤫. Read the sentence to B. Don’t show it!

2. Student B draws it on paper. Then B says the sentence: “The cat is under the table.”

3. Show your picture. Press 👀 to check. Then change: B rolls, A draws.
