“I am tired.” → She said she was tired. · “Please sit down.” → He asked me to sit down. · “Where do you live?” → She asked me where I lived.

The pattern

Present → past. said, told and asked are past, so the verb goes back too:

am / is → wasare → were“I am tired.” → she was tired
work → workeddon’t / doesn’t → didn’t“I live here.” → he lived there
is cooking → was cookingare cooking → were cooking“We are eating.” → they were eating

💬 Statements: said + sentence · told + person + sentence — She said she was tired. · She told me she was tired.

🙏 Requests: asked + person + (not) to + verb — He asked me to sit down. · He asked us not to be late.

📢 Orders: told + person + (not) to + verb — The teacher told us to be quiet. · She told me not to run.

❓ Wh-questions: asked (me) + wh-word + subject + verb — She asked me where I was. (not where was I · no do / did · no ?)

👍 Yes / no questions: asked (me) + if + subject + verb — He asked me if I was tired.

Change the people words: I → he / she · we → they · you → I (when they talk to you) · your → my.
