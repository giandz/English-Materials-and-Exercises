Was and Were — A Life Story

Mia tells Omar about her grandmother.

Before you read

Read these three questions first, then look for the answers in the conversation.

👵 Talking about a grandmother’s life
👩‍🦰
Mia
My grandmother was an amazing woman. She was a nurse for forty years.
👨‍🦱
Omar
Where was she born?
👩‍🦰
Mia
She was born in Lima, in 1935. Her parents were farmers.
👨‍🦱
Omar
Was she the oldest child?
👩‍🦰
Mia
No, she wasn’t. She was the youngest. There were seven children in the family!
👨‍🦱
Omar
Seven! That was a big family. Were they all girls?
👩‍🦰
Mia
No. Five were girls and two were boys. Life was not easy then.
👨‍🦱
Omar
When was she in Chile?
👩‍🦰
Mia
From 1960 to 1972. My mother and my uncle were born there.
👨‍🦱
Omar
So you are half Chilean! Was your grandmother happy there?
👩‍🦰
Mia
She was very happy. But her favourite place was always Peru.
👨‍🦱
Omar
My grandparents were from Egypt. I was born here, but they weren’t.
