Don’t Say the Word!

Explain the word with who, which, that or where. Can your partner guess?

How to play (pairs, or teacher + student):

1. Student A rolls 🎲 and opens 🤫. Only A looks! Explain the word — don’t say it: “It’s a person who takes photos at weddings.”

2. Student B guesses: “A photographer!” Too hard? A gives another clue.

3. Press 👀 for help. Then change: B rolls, A guesses.
